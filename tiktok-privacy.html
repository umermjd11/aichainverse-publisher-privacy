Privacy Policy – AIChainVerse Publisher for TikTok

Last updated: 26 November 2026

AIChainVerse Publisher for TikTok is a private tool used by the owner of the @aichainverse TikTok account to upload AIChainVerse's own educational videos and captions to that account, using TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no login for other users.

What data the app uses

The app signs in only to the @aichainverse account, with the owner's approval, and uses these TikTok scopes:

  • user.info.basic: to confirm it is signed in to @aichainverse.
  • video.upload and video.publish: to upload AIChainVerse's own videos and read their publishing status.

The access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.

What data the app does not use

  • It does not use TikTok Login for anyone other than the @aichainverse account.
  • It does not read, store or analyse other users' profiles, videos, messages or comments.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Data deletion

Because the app does not collect data about other people, there is no user data to delete. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings, and the stored tokens are then deleted. If you believe the app holds any data about you, message @aichainverse on TikTok and it will be reviewed and deleted.
