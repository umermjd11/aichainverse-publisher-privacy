Terms of Service – AIChainVerse Publisher for TikTok

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What the app is

AIChainVerse Publisher for TikTok is a private publishing tool. It uploads AIChainVerse's own educational videos and captions about AI, Web3, blockchain and data science to the @aichainverse TikTok account. It is operated by AIChainVerse's owner for that account only.

Who may use it

Only the owner of the @aichainverse TikTok account. The app has no public sign-up and does not act on anyone else's account.

How it publishes

  • Every post is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded, with a preview of the video, its caption and its privacy setting.
  • The app uploads only content AIChainVerse made or has the right to publish.
  • The app does not add watermarks or promotional logos to content beyond AIChainVerse's own branding.
  • Use also follows TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Developer Terms.

Data

How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: it uses only the access token of the @aichainverse account and collects no data about other people.
